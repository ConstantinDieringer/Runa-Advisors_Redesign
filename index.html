WHAT WE DO

Our Five Elements of Value Proposition.

Runa Advisors connects liquid wealth management, operating know-how, transactions, wealth planning and direct investments into one family-office perspective.

01 Wealth & Asset Management Liquid assets

Independent coordination of banks, managers, reporting and risk view for the family’s liquid wealth.

 02 Operational Hands-on support

Interim CFO/CRO, active board support, restructuring, carve-outs, IPO preparation and trade-sale readiness.

 03 Transactional Major transitions

M&A, IPO, trade sale or merger support from buyer outreach to negotiation, closing and proceeds management.

 04 Wealth Planning Legacy & structure

Succession, next generation, legal, tax, trust, fiduciary and financing topics coordinated with specialists.

 05 Direct Investments Private opportunities

Runa Direct Investments, club deals, co-investments and active representation of direct portfolios.
