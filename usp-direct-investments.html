Direct Investments

A direct investment platform for curated club deals, co-investments, independent due diligence, private asset allocation and active portfolio representation.

Club Deals & Co-Investment

Curated family-office and entrepreneurial deal flow creates access to peer-to-peer private opportunities.Runa organises club deals and co-investment opportunities tailored to the client’s criteria, including active direct investment management, shareholder representation and board or C-level involvement at investee companies.

Private Equity & Venture Capital

Private equity and venture capital exposure is implemented through direct investments and fund commitments.Runa supports strategic asset allocation, advisory, structuring, financing and execution, including club deal organisation and co-investor introductions through the Runa network.

Real Estate Financing & Structuring

Real estate investments are structured around financing, governance and co-investor alignment.Support covers advisory, structuring, financing and execution of direct real estate investments, including club deals and co-investment vehicles with other family offices.

Independent Due Diligence

Independent diligence helps families assess private asset transactions before committing capital.Support covers PE, VC, real estate and private debt opportunities, including transaction structuring, full documentation and investment committee process support.
