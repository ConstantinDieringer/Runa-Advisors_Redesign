Direct Investments

A direct investment platform for curated club deals, co-investments, special situations, due diligence and active portfolio representation.

Club Deals & Co-Investments

Curated deal flow gives families access to private opportunities beyond standard markets.The focus is on peer-to-peer direct investments, club deals and co-investment opportunities tailored to individual criteria and family-office preferences.

Special Situations

Special situations combine capital access with hands-on operational judgment.Turnaround and distressed cases bring together the right investors, management capability and execution discipline where timing and operational experience matter most.

Due Diligence

Independent diligence helps families assess private opportunities before committing capital.Support covers PE, VC, real estate and private debt transactions, including structuring, documentation and investment committee process preparation.

Direct Investment Management

Direct portfolios need active ownership, not passive monitoring.Runa supports shareholder representation, board-level involvement or C-level contribution where investee companies require experienced ownership presence and clear governance.

Private Equity / Private Debt

Private asset exposure is structured around strategy, access and execution quality.Strategic allocation can include direct investments and fund commitments, supported by advisory, structuring, financing and execution for private asset transactions.

Venture Capital & Real Estate

VC and real estate opportunities are assessed with structure, timing and governance in mind.Advisory includes structuring, financing and execution of direct investments, club deal organisation and co-investment vehicles across private growth and real assets.

Exclusive network access

The network creates access where trust and relevance matter more than volume.Access is built around family office co-investors, PE, VC and private debt funds, selected hedge fund partnerships and discreet asset sales.
